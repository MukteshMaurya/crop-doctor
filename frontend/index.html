AI Plant Disease Detector

Upload a clear photo of a plant leaf and the AI model will identify the crop or plant type.

How it works

  1. Upload a leaf photo (drag & drop or browse).
  2. Press Detect Plant Disease to send it to the AI model.
  3. View the predicted crop/plant type, confidence, and top-3 predictions.

The deployed model (unified_plant_resnet50.pth) classifies crop / plant type across 17 classes (apple, banana, blueberry, cherry, citrus, corn, grape, other crops, peach, bell pepper, potato, raspberry, soybean, squash, strawberry, tomato, wheat). It does not diagnose specific diseases — see the disclaimer below.
